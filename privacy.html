📋 Privacy Policy

We Keep Kids' Info Out Of Our Hands

At Micoplay, protecting privacy isn't just a policy — it's how Mico World is built.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

📜 About This Policy

Micoplay builds safe, fun, and creative digital experiences for children. This Privacy Policy explains how Micoplay ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information across our mobile application Mico World: Creative Kids Game (the "App") and our official website (the "Site").

We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of both children and parents. We design our apps to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable global data protection laws.

🛡️ Our Child Privacy Commitment

We believe children should be able to explore, build, and play in a safe environment without being tracked or targeted.

  • No Personal Accounts or Profiles: We do not require accounts, user sign-ups, or login credentials to play the App.
  • No Direct Personal Data Collection from Children: We do not collect names, phone numbers, physical addresses, photos, microphone audio, or exact location data from children.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: We do not display third-party advertisements or use behavioral tracking ads inside our apps.
  • Local Data Storage: All gameplay progress, creations, levels, and user settings are stored locally on your device. We do not store or transmit gameplay creations to our external servers.

🔬 Information We Collect & How It Is Used

A. Inside the App (Mico World: Creative Kids Game)

To maintain app performance and improve the user experience, we collect minimal non-personal data:

  • Anonymous App Analytics (Google Firebase): We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect aggregated, anonymous device metrics (e.g., device model, operating system version, app launch count, crash logs, and general gameplay performance). Firebase Analytics helps us find bugs, stabilize performance, and understand general feature popularity. This data cannot be linked to individual children.
  • In-App Purchases (IAP): All payment processing for full-version unlocks or content packs is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play Store). We never collect, see, or store credit card or payment information. All IAP interactions are protected behind a Parental Gate (e.g., solving a math problem or age-verification pop-up) to prevent unauthorized child purchases.

B. On Our Website & Future Web Demos

  • Waitlist & Updates (Parents & Adults Only): If you sign up on our website for updates or waitlist access for future web versions of our games, we collect the email address you voluntarily provide. We use this strictly to send news and updates regarding Micoplay. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • General Web Analytics: Our website may collect basic, anonymous web usage analytics (such as page views and browser type) to help us optimize site performance.

🤖 AI & Creative Transparency

At Micoplay, we utilize modern AI creative tools alongside traditional software development to design visual, audio, and gameplay mechanics.

Every visual asset, audio element, and game feature created with AI assistance undergoes rigorous review, planning, and curation by human designers and educators at Micoplay. This guarantees that all content presented in our games is 100% child-safe, age-appropriate, wholesome, and free from harmful or inappropriate elements.

🔗 Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal data. We only share non-personal diagnostic data with trusted infrastructure providers solely to operate our service:

  • Google Firebase: Anonymous app telemetry, crash reporting, and analytics.
  • Apple App Store & Google Play: In-app purchase verification and distribution.

These service providers process data under strict data privacy agreements and are restricted from using data for any other purpose.

👪 Parental Rights and Control

Parents and legal guardians retain full control over their family's data. You have the right to:

  • Contact us to inquire about any data processed through our website or services.
  • Request the complete deletion of your email address from our web waitlists/mailing lists.
  • Manage or disable In-App Purchases on your device via your operating system's parental controls (iOS Screen Time or Google Play Family Link).

To exercise any parental rights, please contact us at hello@micoplaygames.com.

🔐 Security & Data Retention

We implement technical safeguards to ensure that analytics and web contact records are held securely. Because gameplay data is saved locally on your device, deleting or uninstalling the App will remove all local app data permanently.

🔄 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, new legal requirements, or web features. We will publish the updated version on our official website with a revised "Last Updated" date.
