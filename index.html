🚧 Early Stages · Our Roadmap

Growing a Safer, Brighter Playground for All Ages

Micoplay is at a very early stage of development. Today we focus on a safe, ad-free creative playground for curious kids — intuitive, open-ended pretend play, coloring, drawing and hidden-object discovery.

Our future goal is an open playground that runs right in your browser: a pretend-play and storytelling creativity tool — not just for kids, but for grown-up artists to create, share and bring their own worlds and stories to life.

A web version of Mico World is on the official roadmap. Subscribe below to follow our progress and get early playtest invitations.

🔒 Ad-free. No behavioral tracking. A safe digital playground for families.
